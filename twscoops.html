How it works

Sixteen choices, one fresh batch.

Every order is created live with your preferred sweetener, flavour, add-ins and toppings — all churned into a completely unique gelato while you choose.

01

Pick the scoop count

Choose how many scoops you want your cup to hold before the machine starts your custom batch.

02

Select sweetener

Choose from Sugar, Stevia, Honey, or Maple Syrup to tailor the taste and texture.

03

Choose flavour, add-ins and toppings

Your gelato is being churned live as you pick flavours, mix-ins and toppings, then dispensed into a copper cup.

Why it feels different

Freshly churned gelato is smoother because it is made at the moment of service.

Freshly churned gelato develops a finer, silkier texture because it is frozen while moving, which limits large ice crystals and keeps the body creamy and soft. That means the gelato can taste richer and more vivid without needing chemical stabilizers just to hold its form.

Serving it in a copper cup also adds clear benefits over paper: copper feels more premium, stays cooler for longer, and helps turn each order into a memorable experience instead of a disposable one.
