How it works

Three steps, one fresh batch.

No two cups are made the same way twice unless you want them to be. Every order is measured and mixed on the spot.

01

Choose your mix

Pick a base and add-ins on the touchscreen, then set the ratio between them. Save it for next time if you like it.

02

Machine blends live

Precision pumps measure and combine your ingredients in front of you — nothing sits pre-mixed waiting in a tub.

03

Get your cup

Dispensed fresh, exactly as you set it. The machine rinses its lines before the next order starts.

Where it fits

One machine, a few good homes for it.

We're choosing our first pilot spots deliberately. Here's what we're looking at.

Home counter

A compact unit for people who make the same few drinks or bowls constantly and want them exact, every time.

Office pantry

One machine, many preferences — nobody has to compromise on ratio or defend their weird combo.

Campus dining

High turnover, high variance in taste. Built to keep up without a line backing up behind it.

Pop-ups & events

A live demo of the product doubles as the draw — people watch their order get made from scratch.
